Common Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about Ashlar system windows, installation specifications, custom anodized finishes, and weather performance ratings.

Ashlar system windows feature an industry-leading 14mm ultra-slim vertical interlock profile, concealed heavy-duty stainless steel sub-floor tracks, and custom multi-chamber thermal breaks. They deliver panoramic glass-to-frame ratios without compromising on structural wind load (certified up to 220 km/h) or acoustic sound insulation (38 dB rating).
Our profile dynamics are available in premium anodized and powder-coated finishes, including Soft Silver Anodized, Anodic Black Matte, Warm Bronze, and Champagne Satin. All surface finishes undergo rigorous Qualanod and Qualicoat testing for maximum UV resistance and coastal anti-corrosion durability.
Ashlar tracks are embedded flush into concrete floor slabs, creating a zero-threshold transition between indoor spaces and outdoor patios. Integrated sub-floor drainage channels quietly route heavy rainfall away from the living area to guarantee complete water-tightness during severe storms.
